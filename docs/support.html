Getting started

On first launch, JotMoney asks for one account and one transaction. After that, the home screen shows Safe to Spend — the amount you can safely spend today — plus today's transactions.

To log something, type a merchant and an amount into the bar at the bottom, for example coffee 5.5, and press return. JotMoney fills in the category and account for you.

Where is my data stored?

By default, everything is stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. There is no JotMoney account and no JotMoney server. iCloud Sync is optional and off by default; when you turn it on, data syncs through your own private iCloud database.

Subscriptions and purchases

Pro is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, a Family yearly subscription, or a one-time BYO Lifetime purchase.

Manage or cancel: open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad → tap your name → Subscriptions → JotMoney. Cancelling stops future renewals; the current period stays active until it ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore purchases: open JotMoney → Settings → Restore Purchases. This also works after reinstalling or switching devices with the same Apple Account.

Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

AI features

Quick entry works fully offline with a deterministic parser. Optional AI features — receipt scanning and Money Coach — can use the built-in Pro quota, Apple Intelligence on supported devices, or your own API key from any OpenAI-compatible provider. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only and is never sent to us.

Duplicates and corrections

JotMoney flags a possible duplicate when the amount, date window, and merchant look alike. You can merge the entries or keep both. Nothing is ever deleted silently — archived entries stay in your data and are excluded from totals.

Export

Ledger → menu (… ) → Export CSV. The file contains date, type, merchant, category, amount, and source, and opens in Numbers or Excel.

Accessibility

JotMoney supports VoiceOver and Dynamic Type. If you hit a screen that does not read well, email us with the device model and iOS version.
